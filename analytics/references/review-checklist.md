# Analytics review checklist

## Integrity

- Are data source, version, cleaning decisions, and analysis code or steps traceable?
- Does every result have an outcome definition, timepoint, denominator, and unit?
- Are exclusions, deviations, missingness, failures, use errors, and adverse events visible?

## Analysis

- Does the method match the outcome, study design, and analysis unit?
- Are estimates accompanied by uncertainty when used for inference?
- Are repeated, clustered, paired, or site-level data handled appropriately?
- Are adjustments, multiplicity, subgroup analyses, and exploratory analyses disclosed?
- Is language calibrated to pilot, feasibility, bench, usability, or clinical-performance evidence?

## Visualisation

- Does the chart show distribution/individual data where useful rather than conceal them?
- Are scale, units, error bars, legends, and reference lines unambiguous?
- Does the figure avoid implying causality, clinical benefit, or approval beyond the data?

## Delivery note

End analytical work with: data limitations, analytic assumptions, unverified inputs, visualisation caveats, and the next required reviewer (for example, investigator, biostatistician, human-factors expert, or quality/regulatory lead).
