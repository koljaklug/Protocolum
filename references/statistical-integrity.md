# Statistical integrity

- Define the estimand or decision question before selecting analyses.
- Report the analyzed population, denominator, missing data, exclusions, protocol deviations, and follow-up time.
- Pair estimates with uncertainty (for example, confidence intervals) whenever inference is presented; do not rely on p-values alone.
- Describe exploratory analyses as exploratory. Small pilots are usually not definitive efficacy studies.
- Pre-specify feasibility progression criteria where possible; do not rewrite them after results without disclosure.
- For paired, clustered, repeated, diagnostic, or device-performance data, use analysis methods appropriate to the design and unit of analysis.
- Report failures, adverse events, usability errors, and unsuccessful tests alongside favorable outcomes.
- Do not calculate a numeric sample size without outcome type, effect/precision target, alpha or confidence level, power where applicable, allocation/design, and attrition assumption.
