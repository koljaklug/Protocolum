# Intake requirements

Ask only for details that are essential to the requested artifact. If the user wants a template or outline, provide it with clearly labeled fields.

## Small clinical study

- research question and study type
- population, setting, sites, and recruitment window
- intervention/exposure/comparator
- primary outcome, measurement method, and timepoint
- planned or analyzed sample and participant flow
- safety monitoring and adverse-event information when relevant
- ethics, consent, and registration status

## Conference abstract or proceedings

- meeting and submission format (word count, headings, deadlines, figures allowed)
- study question and design
- sample and result data, including denominators
- required disclosure, authorship, ethics, and funding information
- presentation type and target audience

## Device prototype study

- device/prototype description and intended use
- development stage and target users
- evidence type: bench, simulated use, usability, feasibility, or clinical evaluation
- environment, test protocol, comparator/acceptance criteria, and measured endpoints
- sample/test count, failures, deviations, harms, and limitations
- risk controls, oversight status, and known regulatory/quality constraints

## Results drafting

Never generate numerical findings without: analyzed sample/test count, denominators, metric definition, timepoint, and the observed values. Include adverse events, failures, exclusions, and missing-data handling where applicable.
