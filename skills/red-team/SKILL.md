# Protocolum Red Team

Read `../../core/evidence-ledger.md`, `../../core/claim-boundaries.md`, `../../references/quality-gates.md`, and `../../analytics/references/review-checklist.md` when analyses or figures are present.

Create `templates/gap-register.md`. Separate must-fix integrity/scientific/submission blockers from clarity improvements. For every issue, name why it matters, the missing evidence or decision, a concrete correction, and the appropriate owner. Do not rewrite facts merely to eliminate a weakness; preserve limitations and uncertainty.
