# Upstream acknowledgement

Protocolum incorporates and extends the clinical medical paper workflows from
[MedPen](https://github.com/Neuro-S/MedPen), created by Neuro-S and licensed
under the MIT License.

Protocolum preserves MedPen's reusable manuscript-writing, translation,
submission, reporting, and quality-control material, then adds guided project
navigation, device-prototype evidence boundaries, analytical traceability,
visualisation specifications, and a prioritized gap-review workflow.
