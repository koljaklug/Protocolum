# Quality gates

Before delivery, assess each item.

1. **Privacy:** source material is de-identified and no clinical advice is presented.
2. **Traceability:** every factual statement and number maps to a user input or is labeled as an assumption/need.
3. **Study completeness:** design, population, endpoint, timepoint, denominator, and limitations are clear.
4. **Safety and harms:** adverse events, test failures, exclusions, and deviations are neither omitted nor minimized.
5. **Evidence level:** device claims match the actual evidence ladder.
6. **Statistical integrity:** no unsupported inference; uncertainty and missingness are reported appropriately.
7. **Submission fit:** word count, headings, disclosures, anonymization, and current organizer/journal requirements need verification where relevant.
8. **Actionability:** output names the next review owner and outstanding decision.
