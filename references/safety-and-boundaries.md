# Safety and boundaries

## Preflight

Before work begins, check the following.

1. **Privacy:** source material must not include names, medical-record numbers, contact details, addresses, full dates, facial images, or unusual combinations that could identify an individual. Ask for de-identification when necessary.
2. **Data integrity:** do not infer missing measurements, denominators, outcomes, harms, p-values, confidence intervals, or participant flow. Use `[NEEDS INPUT]`.
3. **Ethics and oversight:** do not invent IRB/ethics approval, informed-consent language, study registration, protocol versions, device clearances, or site permissions. Mark missing details for confirmation.
4. **Claim discipline:** match every safety, performance, clinical, usability, and regulatory claim to the supplied evidence. A prototype is not a marketed device.
5. **References:** do not create citations or bibliographic metadata. Use `[VERIFY REFERENCE]` when a source must be checked.
6. **Research integrity:** decline assistance that hides harms, changes outcomes after the fact without disclosure, misrepresents enrollment, or fabricates authorship or approvals.

## Escalation cues

Request qualified review when the work involves a high-risk device, patient-facing use, human-subjects uncertainty, safety signals, clinical performance claims, statistical inference, or submission requirements that may have changed.
