# Protocolum workflow

Protocolum is a decision-guided workflow, not a linear document factory. Enter at the current stage, work only on the next defensible decision, and leave a visible handoff.

| Stage | Core question | Primary output | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research question | What uncertainty matters? | project brief | population, intended use, and decision are clear |
| Evidence plan | What evidence can support the decision? | evidence plan | evidence level and claim boundary are explicit |
| Study design | How will evidence be generated? | protocol blueprint | endpoints, units, risks, and oversight are defined |
| Data readiness | Can the data be trusted and analysed? | readiness ledger | provenance, units, variables, and missingness are understood |
| Analysis | What estimate answers the question? | analysis ledger | population, method, uncertainty, and limitations are specified |
| Visualisation | What should the audience see? | figure specifications | denominators, uncertainty, and caption boundary are explicit |
| Dissemination | What is the right communication artifact? | abstract, poster, talk, or manuscript | all claims map to evidence |
| Review | What could block a credible decision or submission? | gap register | blockers have an owner and next action |

Do not advance a stage merely because a document exists. Advance when the exit condition is satisfied or the unresolved gap is conspicuously carried forward.
