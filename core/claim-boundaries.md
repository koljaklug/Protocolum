# Claim boundaries

Calibrate the language to the evidence actually available.

| Evidence available | Appropriate language | Do not claim |
|---|---|---|
| Bench verification | met the specified technical criterion under stated test conditions | clinical benefit, clinical safety, or real-world performance |
| Simulated use / usability | task performance or use errors were observed in the stated scenario | safe use in clinical practice or effectiveness |
| Feasibility pilot | recruitment, workflow, adherence, or implementation was feasible in this setting | definitive efficacy or generalisability |
| Small clinical study | observed association or estimate with uncertainty | causal or practice-changing conclusion without design support |
| Confirmatory evidence | result supported under the specified analysis plan | claims beyond the studied population, outcome, or timeframe |

When uncertain, narrow the claim rather than strengthening the evidence.
